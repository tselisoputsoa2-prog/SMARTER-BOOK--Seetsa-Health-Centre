About Smarter-Book

Smarter-Book is a hospital appointment booking system for Seetsa Health Centre in Ha Seetsa, Leribe. It makes booking less stressful and more convenient for patients and nurses.

The problem

Booking the traditional way means walking long distances, waiting in long queues, and sometimes arriving after operating hours and not being served. The centre is in an isolated rural setting, far from residential communities.

How we researched it

We ran interviews and surveys with three people: an elderly patient who visits regularly, a middle-aged patient, and a member of hospital staff. We asked about:

  • Hospital operating hours
  • Appointment reminders
  • Existing ways of booking
  • Security and storage of patient information
  • Features for different age groups
  • Challenges of travelling to the hospital
  • Booking and rescheduling

What we found, and what we built

Hard to reach and book

The clinic is far away and public transport is limited, which hits elderly patients hardest. Smarter-Book lets you book from home.

Inconvenient operating hours

Hours end at lunch, and delays on the road can make patients arrive too late. You can book any time and choose a morning slot.

No appointment reminders

Patients forget their dates. You choose when an SMS reminder is sent.

Different ages and technology skills

The site has simple navigation, three text sizes, large clear buttons, and phone booking for those who can’t use it.

Records can be lost

Paper records get misplaced. The design uses an organised, secure digital record instead.

Rescheduling needs a visit

Patients had to travel to the hospital to change a booking. You can reschedule or cancel online.

Usability testing

Use this checklist while at least three people try the website. Give each participant the same six tasks. Observe what happens and record the result after each task.

Use participant numbers rather than recording people's private information. Do not mark a task complete until you have observed the participant trying it.

1. Register as a patient

Create a patient account using the Register option, then return to the website.

2. Search for a medical service

Open Services and find a service, for example General check-up or Family planning.

3. Book an appointment

Choose a service, select an available date and time, and submit a booking.

4. Reschedule an appointment

Open Reschedule, select an existing appointment and choose another available date and time.

5. Cancel an appointment

Open Cancel, select an appointment and confirm the cancellation.

6. Find telephone assistance

Open Contact and locate the telephone booking assistance information.

Results are saved in this browser only. Save one record for each participant.
